Manual upload

Flash a file from your computer, exactly named: firmware.bin, firmware.signed.bin, firmware.debug.bin, firmware.debug.signed.bin or rfid.txt (bulk RFID whitelist). No spiffs.bin needed since 3.6.0-RC1; signed files verify as original since 3.6.2.

Note: file upload only works over http:// connections (not https).
